# Sources · economic-evaluation

The method is cited; course material is not reproduced.

## Courses (Industrial Engineering)

| Course | What it brings to the method |
|---|---|
| Finance | time value of money, annuities, NPV, IRR, real and nominal rate |
| Technical Project Evaluation workshop | the technical study as input to the flow (size, investments, operating costs), structure of the pure project cash flow |
| Economics | opportunity cost, sunk cost, incremental analysis (what goes into the flow and what does not) |

## Bibliography

- Sapag Chain, N., Sapag Chain, R. and Sapag Puelma, J. M. *Preparación y evaluación de proyectos*
  (6th ed.). McGraw-Hill, 2014. Ch. 1 and 2 (the project as an investment decision and its
  evaluation); the chapters on building cash flows, evaluation criteria and sensitivity analysis.
  Structure of the "Chilean-style" flow (non-cash charges, book value, market salvage value,
  working capital) and the critical value per variable.
- Blank, L. and Tarquin, A. *Ingeniería económica* (7th ed., Spanish edition of *Engineering
  Economy*). McGraw-Hill, 2012. ISBN 978-607-15-0761-7. Ch. 5 (present worth), ch. 7 (rate of
  return of a project and multiple rates when the flow changes sign more than once), ch. 13
  (payback period), ch. 16 (depreciation), ch. 17 (after-tax analysis), ch. 18 (sensitivity
  analysis).
- Course notes *Estudios Técnicos de Proyecto*, version 9.0, 2011 (Industrial Engineering
  course). Size, location and investments of the technical study, which feed the flow.
- Pindyck, R. and Rubinfeld, D. *Microeconomía* (8th ed., Spanish edition of *Microeconomics*).
  Pearson, 2013. Opportunity cost and sunk costs (general reference for the Economics course; not
  course material).

## Country notes · Chile (`country: CL`)

- Income Tax Law (Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta, DL 824), art. 31 no. 5 and 5 bis: normal and
  accelerated depreciation.
- Servicio de Impuestos Internos (SII), table of useful lives of fixed assets (Resolución Ex.
  no. 43 of 2002 and its amendments): source of `useful_life` and `accelerated_life`.

Other countries: their tax authority's depreciation table plays the same role; nothing else in
the method changes.

The tax rate, the discount rate and the horizon are **not** written here: each company's profile
gives them.
