## Standards

| Standard | What it sets for this method |
|---|---|
| IAS 2 *Inventories* (IASB; NIC 2 in Spanish) | For financial statements inventory is valued by absorption: fixed production overhead is allocated on normal capacity. Variable costing is a management tool, not a reporting one |
| Chile · Value Added Tax Law (Ley sobre Impuesto a las Ventas y Servicios, DL 825), tax credit | Country-specific note (`country: CL`): with an invoice, the purchase IVA is recovered; that is why the margin is computed net against net. Any VAT system with input tax credit works the same way |
