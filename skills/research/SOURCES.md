## Bibliography

  information) and on the sensitivity of the assumptions.
- Booth, W. C., Colomb, G. G., Williams, J. M., Bizup, J. and FitzGerald, W. T. *The Craft of
  Research* (4th ed.). University of Chicago Press, 2016. Primary, secondary and tertiary
  sources; evaluating the reliability of a source; telling claim, evidence and warrant apart.

## Country-specific note · Chile (`country: CL`)

Chilean regulations that often appear in financial gaps:

- Ley 18.010, arts. 6 and 6 bis: current interest and maximum conventional interest; the CMF
  publishes the certificate every month in the Diario Oficial and it applies from its publication.
- Ley 20.285 on access to public information: the transparency request as an action that closes a
  gap with no published source.
